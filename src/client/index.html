How to Fly

Your plane always flies forward — steer it!

Steering:

A / D - Bank left & right

↑ / ↓ - Climb & dive (fly OVER buildings, or crash into them!)

Speed:

W - Faster | S - Slower

Shift - Boost (drains energy)

Combat:

Space - Shoot | R - Barrel roll dodge

Win:

Capture windmills by circling them, and shoot down the enemy team.

Tip: Fly through glowing tulip rings for power-ups! 🌷
