Settings

Low disables shadows and bloom for older laptops. Ultra widens the view distance and sharpens shadows. Press P in flight for photo mode and C to save a screenshot.

Welcome, pilot

Your plane always flies forward. Steer it with the mouse (click the world) or A / D, climb and dive with ↑ / ↓.

Win

Follow the yellow marker to a windmill and fly inside its big glowing circle until the bar fills. Owned windmills score every few seconds. Shooting down enemy planes scores too.

Relax

Bots leave you alone for your first minute in the air, and we'll walk you through the controls once you're airborne.
